# Privacy notice

**Last updated:** 26/09/2026

quietkeys is built by Ashh66, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. This notice explains what happens to your information when you visit the quietkeys website or join the waitlist.

The short version: the only thing collected is your email address, if you choose to give it, and it's used for one thing only.

---

## The quietkeys app

The quietkeys app itself collects nothing. It never connects to the internet, has no accounts, and sends no analytics or crash reports. Your vault stays on your computer.

## The website

- No analytics, no cookies, no tracking scripts and no third-party fonts or embeds.
- The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to run and protect its service. I don't have access to those logs. GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

## The waitlist

**What's collected:** your email address. When you sign up, the email service also records basic technical details (such as your IP address) to prevent abuse.

**Why:** to send you an email when quietkeys v1 is released. You won't be added to any other mailing list, and your email will never be sold, shared or used for advertising.

**Lawful basis:** your consent, given when you join. You can withdraw it at any time.

**Who stores it:** sign-ups are handled by Buttondown, an email newsletter service based in the United States. This means your email address is transferred to and stored outside the UK. Open tracking and link click tracking are switched off, so emails won't record whether you opened them or which links you clicked. Buttondown's privacy policy: [BUTTONDOWN PRIVACY POLICY LINK]

**How long it's kept:** until you unsubscribe, or at most [3] months after v1 is released, when the waitlist will be deleted. If you'd like to hear about future releases after that, you'll be asked to sign up again separately.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law (the UK GDPR), you can:

- ask for a copy of the information held about you
- ask for it to be corrected or deleted
- withdraw your consent and unsubscribe at any time, using the link in any email or by contacting me

To do any of these, email ashh66.dev@gmail.com I'll respond within one month.

If you're unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

## Changes

If this notice changes, the date at the top will be updated. If a change affects how your email address is used, waitlist subscribers will be told before it takes effect.
